01 /Core idea

Fewer services.
More connection.

Guilds do not need a collection of isolated premium tools. They need features that communicate with each other and adapt to the way a guild actually operates.

One system for the work officers handle every day — clear, extensible and free from unnecessary dependencies.

02 /Current projects

What is being built.

The projects grow iteratively: solve a concrete problem first, then stabilise, connect and expand it step by step.

PROJECT / 01In use
Discord · Guild management

Guild Helper

The central Discord bot for recurring tasks: recruitment tickets, member profiles, guild data, absences and temporary voice channels are managed from one shared foundation.

  • Recruitment with Armory and Warcraft Logs checks
  • Roster and attendance integration with Google Sheets
  • Join-to-create voice channels with personal settings
  • Independent web dashboard without bot restarts for design updates

PROJECT / 03Active development
Addon · Bot · Data flow

Guild Bank

An end-to-end workflow for withdrawals and administration: the in-game addon records activity, exports move into the sheet in a controlled way, and the Discord bot makes the data available to officers in the dashboard.

  • Safe in-game capture and confirmed export
  • Sheet import with unique transaction IDs
  • Dashboard management inside the existing Guild Helper
